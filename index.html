Unapologetically Loud.

130dB is a home for the bold. Built for those who live loud, we honor the raw power of hard rock legends who never turned it down. Named after the threshold where music becomes physical, we exist for those who believe rock should be felt, not just heard.

Reckless by Nature

Fueled by pure energy and passion, we celebrate the bold, the wild, and the unchained. At 130dB, we give a stage to real bands with real grit, because rock was never meant to play it safe. If it’s not electric, it’s not us.

Built for Hard Rock. Nothing Else.

This is a dedicated space for the loud, the heavy, the real.No genre-hopping. No compromises. In a world where the genre’s pushed out of the spotlight, we’re bringing it back - loud, raw, and unfiltered.
