About Us

At 130dB, we are unapologetically loud, fiercely authentic, and driven by the relentless energy of hard rock. We exist to fuel the passion of music lovers who crave unfiltered experiences.

We are the heartbeat of a movement that lives and breathes rock.

We honor the legacy of hard rock while pushing it forward with bold and fresh sounds.

Mission, Vision and Values

Mission

130dB is a high-voltage brand built for those who live loud, embrace the raw energy of live music, and crave an authentic rock experience. Dedicated exclusively to hard rock, we provide a stage for real bands, real instruments, and real fans, keeping the movement alive. Our mission is to create a home where music is reckless, unapologetic, and always electric.

Vision

In a world where hard rock is fading from the mainstream and great bands go unnoticed, we believe it should be easy for fans to find affordable, high-energy concerts and discover new artists, because only then can the underground rock scene's spirit stay alive, loud, and unapologetically raw.
